## Question and scope

Does spaced repetition beat massed practice for long-term retention?

Scope: the "Learning" collection. Sources: library, scholarly.

## Method

Searched the library and scholarly databases for controlled comparisons of spaced vs. massed practice.

| Query | Source | Results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| spaced repetition retention | library | 4 |
| massed vs spaced practice | scholarly | 12 |

## Themes

### Consistent long-term advantage

Multiple studies found spaced repetition outperforms massed practice on delayed recall tests.

### Effect size varies by interval

The advantage grows with longer retention intervals and shrinks for immediate recall.

## Evidence table

| Claim | Sources | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spaced practice improves 1-week retention over massed practice. | [@cepeda2006], [@roediger2011] | strong |
| The effect is smaller for tests given within an hour of study. | [@cepeda2006] | moderate |

## Disagreements

One study (unindexed) reported no difference at very short intervals; the majority disagree.

## Gaps and future work

Little coverage of spaced repetition for motor skills specifically.
