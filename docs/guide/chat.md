# Chat

Ask a question in **Chat**; Veritas searches your library and answers with citations.

## Sources

The chips under the message box choose where it looks for this question:

| Chip | Searches |
|---|---|
| Papers | your Zotero library: metadata, abstracts, your highlights and notes, full text |
| Notes | your Obsidian vault and other note folders |
| Scholarly | scholarly databases such as OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar |
| Web | the open web |
| Valency | Valency Bond, when that connector is connected |

Your reading-radar digests are always available to the chat, whatever chips are on.

## Citations

Every claim cites a paper (`[@key]`) or a note (`[[note]]`). Click a citation and the paper or note opens inside
Veritas. Links to papers open the PDF in your library, else a legal open-access copy, else the abstract.

## Models

Settings > Models chooses the AI for each kind of task: Claude, Codex (ChatGPT) or a local model through an
OpenAI-compatible server. Your library stays on your computer; the question and the passages Veritas found are
sent to the model you choose.

## Other modes

The mode menu next to the chips also offers **Find papers** (a table of candidate papers you can add to Zotero) and
**Report** (a longer write-up across many sources).
